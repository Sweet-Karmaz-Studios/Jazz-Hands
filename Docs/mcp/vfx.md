# Trailer effects

`vfx_apply_preset` puts a timed combination of the impact effects on a moment, given with `at` or as a marker's name: `impact.hit` (a quick punch, a rattle, a flash), `impact.heavy` (inverted impact frames, an overshooting punch, a hard shake, a burst of colour fringing, a white flash) or `impact.boss-intro` (red stark frames, a slow springing zoom, a long rumble). Without `to` they go on a new adjustment clip over everything around the moment; with `to` on one clip or track. Each is an ordinary effect afterwards (`video.shake`, `video.zoom-punch`, `video.flash`, `video.chromatic-aberration`, `video.impact-frame`), its `trigger` on the hit. `vfx_presets` lists them.

The looks are effects to add with `effect_add`: `video.glitch`, `video.crt`, `video.vhs` (with the `gen.date-stamp` generator for a camcorder date), `video.echo` (trails), `video.light-sweep` (a shine across a logo), `video.letterbox`, `video.bloom`, `video.deband`, `video.callout` (a magnified inset), `video.frame` (rounded corners, border and shadow). Motion blur is `clip_set_motion_blur`, `track_set_motion_blur` or `sequence_set_motion_blur`; particles are the `gen.particles.*` generators, placed with `clip_add`.

`vfx_find_static` finds what stands still over a clip's moving picture (a game HUD, a watermark, debug text) as regions in source pixels; `vfx_hide_static` blurs, pixelates, covers with a clean frame (`plate`) or crops them away, finding them itself when not given `regions`. Look at the result with `render_frame` or a contact sheet (`export_contact_sheet` with `times` and `clip`).
